Sound lab

Every interface sound on the site, from sfx.js as it ships: short drum-machine voices (909 and 808 hats, rim, clap, shaker, snare tick, high tom) and plucks and house stabs, all in A minor (A, C, E and G, the notes of Am7, A4 = 440), so any cues heard together are consonant. Opening, switching to Controller, downloading and turning sound on rise; the way back is the same sound, single and softer. Click a variant to hear it and pick it; the arrows play a direction.

The softness is modelled on Raphael Salaja’s Pond UI sounds (very short, a gentle filtered contact on the attack, a trace of a small room); his originals are at the bottom for reference. Each variant is trimmed to its slot’s level against Pond’s tap (K-weighted with a 150Hz high-pass for small speakers, loudest 100ms); Measure re-checks.

The site plays only on a click, tap or key (never hover or scroll), only after the first one, and never while the tab is hidden. Use headphones and a quiet system volume: these are meant to sit under everything.

Picks (and the master level) are saved in this browser only when you press “Use picks on the site”; then the real page on this origin plays them.

Try it on the page’s own controls

The same markup patterns as the site, wired by start(), the same function the site uses: press with a mouse (sounds on press), tap on a phone (sounds on lift, never on a scroll), or use Tab and Enter.
