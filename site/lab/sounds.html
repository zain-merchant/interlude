Sounds

Each sound on the site, on a copy of the element it belongs to. Press the example to hear the sound chosen for it, pick another below it, and open Edit to adjust it. Everything is saved into one object, SOUNDS in sfx.js; the code to paste and a new sfx.js are at the bottom.

Every tuned sound plays notes of the key’s minor 7th chord, so sounds heard together never clash. Opening, Controller, Download and sound on rise; the way back is one softer note.

Result

This is the SOUNDS block in sfx.js, between its SOUNDS:begin and SOUNDS:end lines. Paste it there, or save a whole new sfx.js with it in place.

Reference: Pond’s originals
